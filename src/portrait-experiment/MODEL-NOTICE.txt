BiRefNet Portrait INT4 browser experiment

Original model: https://huggingface.co/ZhengPeng7/BiRefNet-portrait
Original project: https://github.com/ZhengPeng7/BiRefNet

This derivative uses 4-bit matrix weights with original float32 activations.
Large Split/Concat operations are rewritten and final-decoder sampling is
batched in independent channel groups. Learned weights and 1024 input remain
unchanged. No training photos or user photographs are included.

Artifact: birefnet-portrait-int4-web-chunked.onnx
Bytes: 298520795
SHA-256: b0e39ef6eeaf57d16f1aaa6be974330fac861a5f15c6691e62f4b2aa84b317c1

ONNX Runtime Web 1.31.1: https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
Runtime license and third-party notices are included in this directory.
hash-wasm 4.12.0: https://github.com/Daninet/hash-wasm (MIT).
The streaming SHA-256 verifier avoids a second full model buffer.
Lanczos resampling follows Pillow's documented 8-bit algorithm:
https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow/blob/13.0.0/src/libImaging/Resample.c
