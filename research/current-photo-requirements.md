# Current photo requirements and print output
Updated November 1, 2026. This document supersedes the earlier catalog inclusion/counts, shared-template experiment and 300-PPI output descriptions.

## Agreed product scope
The existing country → document picker prepares ordinary tourist/visitor visa photos and ordinary passport-renewal photos. There is no Applying from field. A visible English/Chinese caveat asks users to confirm dimensions and other requirements with their embassy or passport authority before printing. Local differences remain in sourced notes or clearly qualified choices; known material restrictions are visible without opening source details.

Current catalog: **88 choices across 83 countries/regions**, including one Schengen visa choice beneath each of the 29 members. Duplicated common-format choices and specialist study, marriage, diplomatic, employment, residence, private-visa, extension and first-application-only routes have been removed.

## Output behavior
- Every 4×6-inch print sheet renders at **3600×2400 pixels**, maximum JPEG quality, with **600-PPI JFIF metadata** and a 600ppi filename.
- Geometry derives from physical millimetres. Copy capacity and cut positions remain physically unchanged, accurate within one output pixel. Guides remain approximately0.085mm thick (two pixels at600PPI) and preserve their physical dash spacing.
- Available cropped-source resolution is computed from the bounded working image, not inferred from export metadata. Warn below600PPI; do not claim interpolation restores detail or unnecessarily block export.
- Single-photo exports retain source-detail pixel dimensions and existing compression behavior. Their density metadata remains300PPI; this does not set their upload pixel dimensions or printer quality.
- A high-density file is not certification of printing, photo provenance or agency acceptance. Actual iPhone memory, physical printing and receiving-authority acceptance remain manual checks.
