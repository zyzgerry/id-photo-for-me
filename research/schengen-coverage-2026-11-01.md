# Schengen coverage — November 1, 2026
User requested a Schengen visa choice beneath every member country, rather than a standalone country group.

## Result
All 29 current Schengen members have a Schengen visa choice. There is no Schengen pseudo-country. Added 25 choices; Austria, Czechia, France and Romania already had unscoped guidance and reuse it. Existing location-specific choices and national D visa choices remain. Current catalog: **97 choices across 77 countries/regions**. Country-list coverage is not a claim of uniform acceptance by every mission.

## Evidence and limits
- [European Commission country list](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/dae5e071-1564-4a5d-9714-2147f7641781_en?filename=FAQs.pdf) lists the 29 members. Ireland, Cyprus and the United Kingdom are not included.
- [Commission application guidance](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/visa-policy/applying-schengen-visa_en) links the [shared photo leaflet](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/5bb16566-c8c2-4afb-b038-530f488cb72a_en?filename=icao_photograph_guidelines_en.pdf). It specifies width 35–40 mm and face 70–80%, but no fixed photo height.
- [EU Delegation to Kenya](https://www.eeas.europa.eu/kenya/travel-study_en) publishes 45×35 mm passport-size photos for Schengen applications. This corroborates the common portrait format; that delegation's application instructions are not asserted to bind every other consulate.
- Therefore the newly propagated 35×45 mm entries are explicitly named **Schengen visa (common format)**. This is a reusable framing default, not a newly verified universal 45-mm-height rule. The in-app note says local requirements take priority. This distinction supersedes the earlier audit's narrower country-specific-only inclusion policy for these common-format entries, as requested by the user.
- Finland uses **36×47 mm**, rather than copying the default: [current MFA visa instructions](https://um.fi/visa-to-visit-finland) direct applicants to [Finnish Police photo instructions](https://poliisi.fi/documents/25235045/31329600/Passport-photograph-instructions-by-the-police-2020-EN-fixed.pdf); dimensions are corroborated by the [Interior Ministry regulation](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2015/532).
- France retains its [ministry photo sheet](https://france-visas.gouv.fr/documents/d/france-visas/iso_iec_normes_photos_fr): light blue/light grey background; white prohibited.
- Greece retains its distinct [UK GVCW tourist route](https://uk-gr.gvcworld.eu/en/visa-info-tourism), 35×40 mm, alongside the common-format option. A UK applicant should use the UK-specific choice.
- Netherlands: the [visa checklist](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/checklist-schengen-visa-tourism) incorporates [Dutch photo requirements](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/passport-id-card/photo-requirements), including 400 dpi prints and different face measurements. The new choice expressly says the app's 300-ppi sheet does not meet that print-resolution rule; a framing option is not a compliant print claim.
- Bulgaria: [MFA](https://www.mfa.bg/en/services-travel/consular-services/travel-bulgaria/visa-bulgaria/) prohibits non-professional photos/crops of amateur snapshots. The note preserves that requirement; this app cannot certify photo provenance.
