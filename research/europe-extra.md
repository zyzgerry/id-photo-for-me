Required live capture excludes a preset only when printed photos are not accepted or required. All evidence is official government/embassy publication.

## Specific checks and supplementary evidence

- **Finland:** [Police submission FAQ](https://poliisi.fi/submitting-passport-photographs) expressly accepts paper photographs when applying at a police service point and says an online application cannot use them. The linked police PDF confirms this on page 1. Its page 3 says pixels only apply to electronic submissions and millimetres only to paper; gives crown-to-chin 32–36mm, headroom 4–6mm and chin margin 7–9mm. No DPI minimum appears in the PDF. The 36×47mm physical format is also expressly published in the [official decree](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2015/532); its successor is [1168/2016](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2016/1168), whose body was not rendered by the retrieval service. The current police PDF and FAQ are the primary application sources, not a claim that the older decree remains current. 500×653px server output is not advertised by this print preset.
- **Germany:** [Saudi mission tourist checklist](https://saudiarabien.diplo.de/ksa-en/visa-service/2436628-2436628) independently confirms a loose 35×45mm photo. UK FAQ explicitly requires photo-quality paper, so UK is the chosen scope. Do not use this for German domestic passports or ID cards.
- **Lithuania:** [Kaliningrad consulate](https://consulate-kaliningrad.mfa.lt/aktualiausia-informacija/vizos/dokumentai-vizai-gauti/40) independently publishes 35×45mm colour matte white-background photo, within six months. Sovetsk source explicitly says glue to the printed form. No national passport standard is inferred from these visa checklists.
- **Serbia:** [Current ministry visa conditions](https://www.mfa.rs/lat/gradjani/ulazak-u-srbiju/uslovi-za-vizu) confirm 3.5×4.5cm and describe electronic visas separately. The official paper form provides the print evidence. Do not present this as the electronic upload specification.
- **Moldova:** [Current central ministry visa requirements](https://www.mfa.gov.md/ru/content/tipy-viz-perechen-trebovaniy) independently specifies colour 35×45mm across visa categories. The India embassy checklist requests two photos, providing a scoped consular route. Direct India page fetch timed out; central ministry page rendered fully.
- **Azerbaijan:** Tokyo page specifically requests a 3×4cm white-background photo glued to the printed form; direct retrieval intermittently failed but official-domain result provided full checklist. [Migration Code, Articles 36 and 37](https://www.migration.gov.az/content/pdf/M%C9%99c%C9%99ll%C9%99%20ENG%202-edited.pdf) independently requires two colour 3×4cm photos for consular visa issuance. Count differs by route; Tokyo note follows Tokyo. ASAN eVisa is a separate route.
- **Slovenia:** Zagreb’s national-visa checklist says photo 45×35mm (height first); portrait is represented width35/height45. Source refers to ICAO and requires in-person delivery of supporting documents. The broader national [biometric photo PDF](https://www.gov.si/assets/ministrstva/MZEZ/Dokumenti/konzularne-zadeve/oddelek-za-varstvo-interesov-slovencev/Navodila-MNZ-za-biometricno-fotografijo.pdf) specifies 35×45mm, thin glossy white photo paper and no retouching but applies to passports, ID and driving licences, so those passport rules were not silently substituted for the visa. That PDF also forbids self-service booths; no passport preset added.
- **Armenia:** The current Migration and Citizenship Service page expressly allows in-person submission by the applicant **or an authorized representative**, with one colour 35×45mm photo. This is only entry-visa extension at Yerevan, not initial visa issuance or eVisa. No 600dpi digital-upload standard is applied to the paper extension route.

## Follow-up: required prints remain useful alongside live capture

The inclusion boundary was clarified: a mandatory biometric/live-photo appointment does not invalidate a separate printed-photo requirement. The following three presets are included specifically to prepare those paper photographs.

| Preset | Width × height mm | Official source | Required print and limitations |
|---|---:|---|---|
| mx-visa — Visa (Embassy in Baku) | 35 × 45 | [Mexican Embassy in Azerbaijan](https://embamex.sre.gob.mx/azerbaiyan/index.php/foreign-nationals) | One white-background 35 × 45 mm photograph affixed to the printed, signed application form. This prepares the required paper photo; facial/iris/fingerprint capture at the consular appointment remains mandatory under Mexican visa rules. |
| be-visa — Student visa (Helsinki) | 35 × 45 | [Belgian Embassy in Helsinki](https://finland.diplomatie.belgium.be/sites/default/files/2024-07/Student%20Visa_0.pdf) | Two recent colour prints, light uniform background; write your name on the back. For D student visas at Belgian public higher-education institutions in the Helsinki checklist. Paper photos supplement any required biometric appointment and live-photo capture. |
| is-visa — Visa (Embassy in London) | 35 × 45 | [Icelandic Embassy in London](https://www.mfa.is/library/01-Ministries/Ministry-for-Foreign-Affairs/Consular-services/Tourism%20Business%20VFF%20-%20Checklist%20-%20LON%20-%2014.22.pdf) | One passport photograph, white background, maximum 6 months old, submitted with the printed and signed visa application. London tourism/business/family checklist. Paper photographs supplement any required appointment biometrics or live-photo capture. |

- **Mexico:** Baku primary checklist explicitly instructs printing the form double-sided, signing it, and affixing the 35×45mm white-background photo. [National visa guidelines](https://sidof.segob.gob.mx/notas/docFuente/5763837) require electronic facial, iris and fingerprint capture separately. The Baku page is behind bot protection on direct open; the full official-domain search result includes both the numerical size and the affix-to-form instruction. [Japan official instructions](https://embamex.sre.gob.mx/japon/index.php/en/visas-en) independently request a physical colour3×4cm photo with the personal application, demonstrating why the Baku size is labelled by mission. Netherlands instructions forbid home prints/photo-booth photos, and Leamington requires a stamp; these local restrictions are not assumed for Baku.
- **Belgium Helsinki:** The official PDF page1 requests two recent35×45mm colour photos and expressly says to write the name on the reverse, establishing physical prints. Its scope is D student applications at Belgian public higher-education institutions, excluding exchange/Erasmus, internships and private institutions. The administrative precheck is sent as separate PDFs; the back-of-photo instruction remains in the original checklist. No numerical DPI minimum is stated in this visa checklist. Passport/ID400dpi requirements are not silently transferred to visas. Biometric collection must still be completed as instructed by the embassy.
- **Iceland London:** Official MFA-hosted PDF identifies Embassy of Iceland, London and lists one35×45mm white-background photo no older than6months among documents to be submitted with the printed/signed visa forms. [Current central visa supporting-document page](https://island.is/en/get-a-visa/supporting-documents) corroborates physical dimensions. No more-than300ppi threshold or mandatory studio requirement appears in that checklist. Any live capture is supplementary and not represented as replaced by this print.

## Retrieval limitations

Official PDF/page content was opened and inspected beyond snippets wherever retrievable. Some ministry sites (German UK, Moldovan India, Azerbaijani Tokyo) blocked or timed out on direct opens; full official-domain search extraction plus an independent primary rule was used where available. No commercial photo service was used as authority. Search engines contained many fabricated-looking “2026 verified” photo-size sites; none informed the measurements. No numeric head fraction is inferred from generic face percentage unless the source defines crown-to-chin. Only Finland and Luxembourg Bangkok receive head guides.
