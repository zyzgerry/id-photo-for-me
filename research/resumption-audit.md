# Resumption source audit

Checked 2026-10-31. Targeted follow-up to the saved 99-preset/95-country catalog; not a fresh verification of every entry. No source files changed by this audit.

## Result

No blocking contradiction found in the sampled existing routes. Spain remains excluded: central domestic guidance has no numerical print-resolution floor, but an embassy's technical sheet expressly requires720dpi for prints. The applicability of that threshold beyond the mission is unresolved, so the catalog remains99 presets across95 countries/regions.

- **Spain domestic passport:** the current [Interior Ministry procedure](https://www.interior.gob.es/opencms/va/servicios-al-ciudadano/tramites-y-gestiones/pasaporte/procedimiento-de-expedicion/) requests one recent colour 32×26mm photograph, plain white background, frontal face with the full facial oval visible, high resolution and good photographic paper. A separate photograph is unnecessary if the DNI was issued/renewed the same day. The central page supplies no numerical DPI floor or compulsory photographer. [Consolidated passport decree, article 4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2003-13978) independently corroborates size/background.
- **Spain orientation and consular caveat:** the Spanish embassy in La Paz's [official photograph requirements](https://exteriores.gob.es/Embajadas/lapaz/es/Documents/REGISTRO_CIVIL/REQUISITOS%20FOTOGRAFIA.pdf) explicitly define minimum **26mm width ×32mm height**, maximum35×45mm. Its separate quality rule distinguishes scanner input300ppi from printed-photo720dpi. Thus this embassy route must be excluded from a300ppi print claim. Direct PDF retrieval returned403; official-domain indexed full-text extraction supplied these exact technical rules. This is orientation evidence and a mission-specific exclusion, not proof that domestic Spain imposes720dpi.

## High-impact boundaries rechecked

- [Finnish Police submission FAQ](https://poliisi.fi/submitting-passport-photographs) still expressly accepts paper photographs at police service points and excludes them from online applications. The included print route remains supported despite the recommendation to use electronic submission.
- [Dutch government passport photo requirements](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/passport-id-card/photo-requirements) still require minimum400dpi. Retaining the exclusion is consistent with this product's300ppi output.
- [Greek passport authority specifications](https://passport.gov.gr/en/diadikasia-ekdosis/documents/specificationphoto.html) retain1200dpi for digital-camera prints and prohibit inkjet/laser prints. Domestic applications use myPhoto. Do not confuse this excluded passport route with the included UK visa route.
- The live [authorized Greek UK tourist-visa page](https://uk-gr.gvcworld.eu/en/visa-info-tourism) repeats35×40mm in both the checklist and photo specifications; two supplied colour photos remain required alongside biometric capture. The catalog's unusual height is supported, not a35×45 typo.
- The current [France-Visas ministry photo sheet](https://france-visas.gouv.fr/documents/d/france-visas/iso_iec_normes_photos_fr), opened in full, explicitly gives35×45mm and chin-to-crown excluding hair32–36mm. It prohibits white background and specifies light blue/light grey. The saved French preset preserves this critical distinction.
