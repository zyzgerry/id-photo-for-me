# Visa label scope check

Checked2026-10-31. Label-only research for existing presets; no sizes, offerings or code changed.

## Ambiguous European labels

Use **Schengen Visa / 申根签证** for verified short-stay Schengen routes. For a source covering both Schengen and national visas, use **Schengen / National Visa / 申根／国家签证**. Keep mission, application location and specific purpose in the existing scope field. Do not relabel a national D visa as Schengen merely because the issuing country belongs to the Schengen area.

## Other shared regional systems

No existing non-European catalog visa preset is actually sourced to another joint regional visa. Do not change a national visa's label based merely on geography or recognition of a visa issued elsewhere.

- **East African Tourist Visa / 东非旅游签证** is a real shared product for Uganda, Kenya and Rwanda, confirmed by [Uganda Immigration](https://immigration.go.ug/services/east-african-tourist-visa) and [Rwanda's official embassy instructions](https://www.rwandainthenetherlands.gov.rw/services/how-to-apply-for-an-east-african-tourist-visa). The existing catalog has no visa preset for these countries; no label change or new offering is warranted.
- **KAZA UNIVISA / 卡扎统一签证** is a real Zambia/Zimbabwe product, confirmed by [Zambia Immigration](https://www.zambiaimmigration.gov.zm/for-visitors/). Those countries' existing catalog entries are passports, so do not apply the visa name to them.
- Existing Costa Rica and other Americas visa routes are national consular routes, not a separately evidenced joint regional visa. Existing Oman temporary-work and other Middle East routes likewise do not establish a joint Gulf tourist-visa option.

Skipped: unrelated photo-specification revalidation, adding regional products, and universalizing mission-specific print rules. Risk: Schengen membership alone does not establish a particular route's C/D classification or acceptance requirements.

## Other renamed Schengen routes

The [European Commission](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/visa-policy/applying-schengen-visa_en) defines short-stay Schengen visas and distinguishes national long-stay procedures. Italy's [foreign ministry](https://www.esteri.it/en/servizi-opportunita/ingressosoggiornoinitalia/visto_ingresso/) explicitly calls its short-stay visa the Uniform Schengen Visa. The existing [Danish Pakistan checklist](https://um.dk/pakistan/en/travel-and-residence/visa/document-checklists/) and [Swedish Iceland checklist](https://www.swedenabroad.se/en/about-sweden-non-swedish-citizens/iceland/going-to-sweden/visiting-sweden/tourist-visa/) explicitly reference Schengen applications; [Norwegian visitor-visa guidance](https://www.norway.no/en/central-content/en/service-info/visitors-visa-res-permit/visitors-visa/) describes Schengen visits. The [authorized Greek UK tourist route](https://uk-gr.gvcworld.eu/en/visa-info-tourism) is expressly Schengen. Existing Latvia, Hungary, Lithuania, Portugal and Romania entries already identified their Schengen route. The French scientific short-stay purpose remains qualified in the label; its mission scope is retained. None of these naming changes alters the route-specific photo requirements.
