# Asia and Oceania: verified printed-photo presets

Checked 2026-10-31. **24 presets, 21 countries/regions.** Machine-readable companion: `src/catalog-asia.js`, export `ASIA_PRESETS`.

This is a catalog of verified application contexts, not a claim that every application to a country uses the same photo. Printed passport options and printed visa options are distinct. Mission-specific, age-specific and assisted-paper scopes are part of the selectable document names. Digital-only upload instructions are not treated as proof that a photo can be printed for submission.

The product exports a 300 ppi print sheet. No included option has a verified mandatory minimum above 300 dpi. Australia and New Zealand paper passports require special printing processes/materials: preparing the image at home does not make ordinary inkjet output acceptable. No source below was found to mandate an accredited photographer for the included route. Some recommend professional capture.

## Included options and evidence

Dimensions are width × height in millimetres. Exact inch units are converted using 25.4 mm/inch; an authority's explicit rounded millimetre value is retained where given (India 51 mm). Unknown background/recency values are explicitly unknown and have not been borrowed from another country.

## Cross-checks and important implementation limits

- **China:** the [Sydney centre page](https://bio.visaforchina.cn/SYD4_EN/qianzhengyewu/jichuzhishi/changjianwenti/552371235904950309.html) currently says glossy-only, while an [older MFA/Embassy diagram](https://tr.china-embassy.gov.cn/xwdt/201206/P020211023337284464280.pdf) allows glossy or matte. Use the newer scoped requirement: glossy only. The older diagram directly defines 28–33 mm as chin-to-crown and provides the 3–5 mm top gap.
- **Singapore:** the [paper passport form](https://www.ica.gov.sg/docs/default-source/ica/forms/imm_%28e%29_11.pdf) explicitly permits mailing and specifies the print size, age and head measurement. [ICA photo guidelines](https://www.ica.gov.sg/photo-guidelines) require matte/semi-matte paper and explicitly permit mobile-device capture and resizing, while prohibiting enhancement.
- **Bhutan:** the [current MFA application page](https://www.mfa.gov.bt/passport-application/) says ordinary applications go online; offline assistance is only for emergencies or inability to use the system. An [official paper application](https://www.mfa.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/Application-for-PP-or-TD.pdf) independently confirms print paper, glue, age and photo specification. The indexed full current page was inspected; direct fetch timed out. The required national dress is preserved in the note.
- **New Zealand:** the [July 2025 paper adult-renewal form](https://www.passports.govt.nz/__data/assets/pdf_file/0024/54753/PAS300-Application-for-a-NZ-passport-Adult-renewal.pdf) gives the 35 × 45 mm dimensions, non-white background and printer restrictions. [Current passport guidance](https://www.passports.govt.nz/passport-photos) explicitly permits taking the photo with your own device. The form's head rule includes hair, so it is not converted into a crown-to-chin guide.
- Head guides are supplied only where a source explicitly defines crown-to-chin measurements: China, Korea passport, Hong Kong, Australia, Singapore. Japan visa's diagram has a 34±2 mm face measurement, but its boundary wording is insufficiently explicit here, so no guide is encoded. Percentages for Bhutan/Taiwan remain notes.
- `restrictEdits: true` means preserve appearance and background. Sources must still be read for all details, infant exceptions and whether the applicant is eligible for the stated route. Cropping/printing does not certify acceptance.
- The Nepal passport print route is only for newborns/children under five. [Nepal's Department of Passports](https://nepalpassport.gov.np/en/process/what-kind-of-photo-is-required-for-the-passport) says other applicants have photos captured at enrollment.
- Some official checklists give only dimensions and request a recent photograph. Missing age limits, backgrounds and head measurements are deliberately not invented. These entries keep a source link for mission-specific follow-up.

## Validation

A small import/self-check verifies 24 records, 21 unique region codes, unique IDs, positive dimensions, HTTPS source URLs, bilingual notes, the checked date and valid optional head ranges. It does not establish that a visa mission will accept an applicant's particular photo or that a printer preserves physical size; those require actual application/print checks.

## Additional Central Asia check

Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan are included above. Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan were checked but not added: Kyrgyzstan's current [official visa guidance](https://www.ereg.e-gov.kg/get_information.php?lng=en) requests digital photos, and no reliable current printed size was established. Tajikistan's [legacy MFA instructions](https://www.mfa.tj/en/main/view/94/visa-and-consular-issues) say 35 × 45 mm photos printed from a single negative, while the [linked official form](https://mfa.tj/uploads/main/2013/03/visa_application_form_in_english.pdf) instead specifies 30 × 40 mm; current [consular guidance](https://consular.tj/ru/pages/visa-basics/) uses online systems. This inconsistency was not resolved by guessing a size. Turkmenistan was added via its currently linked Washington DC application form; the maximum physical dimensions are explicitly given in inches.
