# Americas printed-photo research

Checked 2026-10-31. 16 presets: 14 sovereign countries and 2 separately identified territories (Anguilla and British Virgin Islands). US is already in the original catalog. Only official government/embassy sources were used. This is dimensional preparation, not a guarantee of acceptance. Local visa standards are deliberately named in the label. Research does not establish that a country permits arbitrary editing; all entries restrict edits.

No head fraction is inferred from a generic “70–80% of face” phrase. Crown-to-chin guides are included only for Canada visa, Anguilla and BVI. Bahamas specifies top of hair, so no crown-to-chin fraction is assigned. Where an official range rather than one size is published, the selected size is inside that range.

## Exclusions and limitations

|---|---|

| Guyana passport | Excluded despite supporting prints: [government renewal portal](https://portal.gov.gy/services/SVC-21C7DA) requires attendance for photo/fingerprint capture; [New York consulate](https://guyanaconsulatenewyork.org/passports/) also expressly requires in-person biometric photo. |
| Barbados passport | Excluded: [Immigration Department](https://immigration.gov.bb/pages/passport.aspx) says photos should be taken by a professional photographer. |
| Jamaica passport | Excluded: [PICA adult renewal instructions](https://www.pica.gov.jm/sites/default/files/Forms/Instruction-Sheet-Adult-Renewal-form-Final.pdf) require professionally produced photographs. General PICA page alone omits that restriction. |
| Saint Vincent and the Grenadines passport | Excluded: [consular guidance](https://svgconsulate.vc/documents/consular/nationals/N-SVG-passport-fees-and-information.pdf) requires commercial-studio high-resolution image and lists paper photographs as unacceptable; older paper forms are not used to override it. |
| Trinidad and Tobago passport | No generic preset: [Toronto renewal instructions](https://foreign.gov.tt/documents/252/Document_7_-_MRP_Renewal_Instructions.pdf) page 1 requests standard Canadian size while page 4 illustrates 5×5 cm minimum; [another official guide](https://foreign.gov.tt/documents/606/Frequently_asked_passport_questions%E5%B8%B8%E8%A7%81%E6%8A%A4%E7%85%A7%E9%97%AE%E9%A2%98.pdf) gives a different minimum. Under-16 Toronto renewals require live capture. A reconciled, current local rule is needed. |
| Mexico | No preset added. [Current visa guidelines](https://sidof.segob.gob.mx/notas/docFuente/5763837) require electronic capture of face/iris/fingerprints; embassy lists still request prints, but they should not be marketed as replacing live capture. |

## Verification notes

All links above were discovered and requested during this research, rather than generated from guessed patterns. Primary pages/PDFs were inspected beyond snippets where the browsing service could retrieve them. Some older official PDFs remain hosted with no clear supersession date; location-specific labels avoid extrapolating them nationally. Print size does not establish permitted background editing, certification, photographic-paper quality or acceptance; retain those requirements in product notes.



## Final audit corrections

Dominica now follows its linked ePassport instructions (35×45mm maximum) instead of the contradictory older webpage. Peru uses the April2026 Guayaquil government checklist (50×50mm; paper submission allowed, do not glue). Bahamas is expressly paper-only. Uruguay was deferred because the source was a general investment-promotion agency rather than a consular policy-owner specification. The old indexed-only Toronto Peru source is no longer used.
