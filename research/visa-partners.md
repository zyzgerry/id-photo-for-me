# Additional printed visa photo routes

Checked 2026-10-31. Two added countries: France and Greece. Companion export: `PARTNER_PRESETS` in `src/catalog-visa-partners.js`.

These options cover identified printed application routes. Required biometric capture remains required; presenting paper photographs does not imply that an applicant can bypass the appointment.

## France — short-stay scientific visa, Libreville

The [Consulate General of France in Libreville checklist hosted by TLScontact](https://static.tlscontact.com/media/ga/lbv/fr/court_sejour_activite_scientifique.pdf) explicitly requests two colour biometric photos measuring 3.5 × 4.5 cm, on a plain light background, taken within six months. It also requires the original signed short-stay application, establishing a paper-document route.

The [current France-Visas Gabon page](https://www.france-visas.gouv.fr/en/gabon) confirms that TLScontact receives applications for the consulate. Its biometrics section says photos may be scanned or taken at application and that children's provided photographs are scanned. Only the personalized France-Visas supporting-document list is authoritative for a particular application.

Crucially, this current government page links the [French-language ministry visa photograph sheet](https://france-visas.gouv.fr/documents/d/france-visas/iso_iec_normes_photos_fr). It explicitly supplies **35 mm width × 45 mm height**, chin-to-crown excluding hair **32–36 mm**, and recent/current likeness under six months. Background must be plain light blue or light grey; **white is prohibited**. These dimensions do not depend on inferring height from an older leaflet that states only width. The head guide is therefore directly supported.

The catalog is scoped to the verified Libreville scientific short-stay route. No compulsory professional photographer or minimum DPI over 300 is stated in those sources.

## Greece — tourist visa through GVCW in the United Kingdom

The live [GVCW UK tourism page](https://uk-gr.gvcworld.eu/en/visa-info-tourism) explicitly identifies its centres as authorized by Greece's UK diplomatic/consular authorities. Both its basic-document list and Photo Specifications section give **3.5 × 4 cm**. The metric dimensions are used; the page's approximate inch equivalent is not mathematically exact and is not used for conversion.

It requires two recent colour photos, at most six months old, white/off-white background, facing forward, forehead/ears visible, non-smiling. Prints must be high quality on photographic paper, preferably non-glossy. The same page separately requires in-person biometrics including a digital facial image, so the note preserves this separate requirement. No studio-only capture or numeric minimum above 300 dpi is stated for the supplied paper prints. No crown-to-chin measurement is given, so no head guide is encoded.

This UK mission size must not be silently replaced with a generic 35 × 45 mm Schengen assumption. Other verified Greek mission sources differ: the [Embassy in Tehran Type D checklist on VFS](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/Greece/Iran/english/pdf/greece-d-visa-checklist-new.pdf) says one colour white-background 35 × 45 mm photo within six months; a [St Petersburg consular checklist on GVCW](https://qa.gvcworld.eu/sites/default/files/paragraph/files/detailed_documents_st.petersburg_062024_en.pdf) requests two 35 × 45 mm prints, one glued and another with a passport number on the reverse. These are evidence of mission differences, not substitutes for UK requirements.

## Excluded routes and boundaries

- **France US student visa:** [current Campus France USA guidance](https://www.usa.campusfrance.org/guidance-for-student-visa-applicants) explicitly says not to take the photo yourself. This scoped restriction must not be hidden by using a generic France option.
- **France passport:** not researched or added here; visa photo authorization does not establish the passport workflow.
- **Greece passport:** [current passport authority specifications](https://passport.gov.gr/en/diadikasia-ekdosis/documents/specificationphoto.html) require at least 1200 dpi for printed photos from digital cameras and prohibit household inkjet/laser prints. These passport-specific restrictions are incompatible with this 300 ppi product. They are not imported into visa rules without an applicable source.
- **Netherlands visa:** the [current tourist visa checklist](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/visa-the-netherlands/checklist-schengen-visa-tourism) requests photos meeting Dutch requirements, 35 × 45 mm, and directs applicants to the [Dutch photo guidance](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/passport-id-card/getting-photo), whose introductory sentence explicitly covers visa applications too. The [visa checklist PDF](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/site/binaries/content/assets/pdfs-engels/checklist-schengen-visa-tourism-en.pdf) expressly incorporates Dutch passport photo requirements; those [requirements specify minimum 400 dpi](https://www.netherlandsworldwide.nl/passport-id-card/photo-requirements). The current checklist also says external service providers take digital photos at appointment instead of requiring supplied paper photos. No verified 300 dpi printed exception was found, so no Netherlands preset is added.
- **France Thailand:** [TLScontact's 2021 guide](https://static.tlscontact.com/media/th/france_visas_guidebook-july2021.pdf) explicitly includes 35 × 45 mm and originals at the appointment; [current France-Visas Thailand](https://www.france-visas.gouv.fr/en/web/france-visas/thailande) confirms TLScontact is still authorized. Not added as another option because the current French photo sheet and Libreville scope already establish one usable French route.

No commercial photo-maker source was used as authority. No blanket "all Schengen countries" size or head measurement is inferred.

## Validation

Import/self-check verifies two records, unique IDs/country codes, expected dimensions, HTTPS sources, bilingual document names/notes, checked date, editing restrictions, and the directly sourced French head guide. Actual prints, application appointments and acceptance were not tested.
