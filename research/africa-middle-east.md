# Africa and Middle East printed-photo catalogue research

Checked: 2026-10-31. Result: **20 presets covering 20 countries**. This is a catalogue of specific published paper application routes, not a promise that every citizen/application uses a printed photograph.

Each included size was found in that country's own government/embassy/consulate publication (Lebanon's embassy-authored checklist is hosted by its application contractor). Inch sizes are converted exactly: 2 inches = 50.8 mm. Portrait formats written height-first as 6 × 4 are stored width-first as 40 × 60 mm. Sources using the familiar local 3 × 4 / 4 × 4 / 4 × 6 photographic notation are normalized to millimetres. No country inherits a regional default.

## Additional evidence and implementation decisions

- Algeria: the [passport/ID application form](https://consulat-metz-algerie.fr/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/Formulaire-psp-biometrique-1.pdf) itself displays 35 × 45 mm; a [2026 consular notice](https://consulat-metz-algerie.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/PERMANENCE_CONSULAIRE_ST_DIZIER_15_02_2026.pdf) still requires two recent white-background prints for passport dossiers. Biometric enrolment can also be required; the print is still a required dossier component. The separate photographic standard explicitly defines chin-to-crown 32–36 mm. The preset uses those millimetre bounds rather than the rounded 70–80% wording.
- Tunisia: the Washington route really specifies **2 × 2 inches**. Tunisia's [e-consulate passport page](https://teh.econsulat.tn/fr/services-de-chancellerie/etablissement-du-passeport) instead writes 4.5 × 3.5 cm; these are mission-specific differences, so the Washington preset is labelled accordingly.
- Egypt: [Doha FAQ](https://cs.mfa.gov.eg/en/QAT/faq) independently requires 4 × 6 white-background unframed prints for passport cases as well (four prints). One visa preset is enough to represent the country; visa scope is two prints.
- South Africa: Toronto's maxi-passport page requires four printed photos and does not mandate a photographer's credential. Its “face 70–80%” instruction is not encoded as crown-to-chin because it does not define the measurement. [Canberra passport instructions](https://dirco.gov.za/canberra/south-african-passports/) expressly say professionally taken; that process was not chosen. [Western Cape guidance](https://www.westerncape.gov.za/service/applying-passport) confirms biometric/smartcard offices capture photos digitally and do not require prints; the Toronto-specific label avoids implying a domestic live-capture use case.
- Zambia audit correction: the legacy Ottawa /DELETE/ PDF is no longer the catalogue source. The [current ministry service page](https://www.mohais.gov.zm/?page_id=1610) explicitly states **38 × 51 mm or 51 × 64 mm for applicants below 16** and requires two recent unmounted prints on a clear background, with one endorsed. The catalogue is narrowed to that under-16 scope and 38 × 51 mm. Adult text is malformed (“38mm x 51mm x 64mm”) and is not used as a precise adult size rule. No minimum DPI or mandatory professional photographer is specified in this checklist.
- Zimbabwe: Ottawa's page requires two 35 × 45 mm prints, embassy fingerprinting/authentication, then onward submission in Zimbabwe. This is not presented as a universal domestic e-passport capture process. [London's page](https://www.zimlondon.gov.zw/?p=5793) mandates studio photos, so that route is excluded.
- Mauritius: 35 × 45 mm is the lower endpoint of an explicitly allowed size range, not an inferred universal size. Crown-to-chin is explicitly 25–35 mm; matt paper is required.
- Turkey: [Karachi's 2025 checklist](https://karachi-cg.mfa.gov.tr/Mission/ShowInfoNote/415169) independently confirms white-background 50 × 60 mm for sticker visas. Los Angeles additionally accepts 2 × 2 inches locally, but one 50 × 60 format avoids adding unnecessary variants.
- Bahrain: the NPRA [in-person issuance checklist](https://www.npra.gov.bh/services/passports/issuance-of-passport/) requires a recent clear front-facing colour 4 × 6 photo with white background. The linked PDF states 4 × 6 cm explicitly and forbids software alteration/enhancement; restrictEdits is therefore required.
- Syria: the Ministry's page was updated 2026-03-29 and requires two printed colour 4 × 4 photos with white background and dark clothing for mission passport renewal. Signature/fingerprint enrolment accompanies the dossier and does not replace those two prints.

## Safety of the catalogue

### Second-pass audit

- Final count remains **20 presets / 20 countries**; no extra countries added from weak cached or contradictory publications.
- Zambia now cites the live ministry checklist and is limited to **under 16**. The old Ottawa PDF is retained only as historical research, not as verified current catalogue evidence.
- Ghana's named honorary consulate is a real office; its [current visa page](https://www.ghanaconsulatear.org/visas/) says it no longer issues visas. The newer [05202025 form](https://www.ghanaconsulatear.org/media/d30bgflc/ghana-visa-application-05202025.pdf) expressly says it can facilitate applications issued by Washington and repeats two 2-inch photos. Updated source and note reflect this distinction. General Ghana mission pages use only “passport-sized”; no universal Ghana print size is claimed.
- South Africa's linked [official photo flyer](https://dirco.gov.za/ottawa/wp-content/uploads/sites/67/2025/04/Passport-ID-Photo-Standards-Flyer-23-April-2009.pdf) requires **professional printing**, while saying home printing is unlikely to be acceptable. Professional photo-lab printing is compatible with this tool's print-sheet output; note now expressly requires it. No mandatory accredited photographer was found in the selected Toronto route. The Toronto-specific white background instruction governs over the general flyer's light-grey/cream examples.
- Tanzania's Kuwait PDF is numeric, but a nearby [visa-fee page](https://www.kw.tzembassy.go.tz/services/visa-fee) refers to cheques for the embassy in Abu Dhabi, UAE, and contains other old/copied instructions. This is not sufficient to treat that paper route as verified current. eVisa numerical size is not converted into a print preset.
- Iran re-search did not produce a usable numerical official current paper size. Saudi's only new precise paper finding was a 2-inch **emergency passage-document** checklist; that falls outside passport/visa scope. Qatar's official passport dimensions remain contradictory. All remain excluded.
- Embassy-like .org pages with replicated generic visa templates (Kuwait, Syria and a non-government Jordan result) were not used. Jordan cites mfa.gov.jo, Syria cites mofaex.gov.sy, and the selected Israel/Turkey/Oman/Bahrain pages use government or official mission domains.

All entries set restrictEdits=true. No beauty filters, invented head-height ratios, automatic background recolouring, or unofficial “ICAO default” values were added. Head bounds appear only for Algeria and Mauritius, where the sources explicitly define chin/crown endpoints. Photo acceptance still depends on pose, lighting, recency, the named mission and actual photographic paper/printing.
