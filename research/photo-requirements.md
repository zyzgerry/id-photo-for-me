# Official printed-photo catalog and benchmark

Checked October 31, 2026. **99 document/application presets across 95 countries and regions**; this is not a claim that both passports and visas are covered for each country. Dimensions are width × height, in mm. Each selectable route includes source, verification date, bilingual scope/notes, and editing restriction. Sources can change; the receiving authority's current checklist controls acceptance.

## IDPhoto4You benchmark

[IDPhoto4You](https://www.idphoto4you.com/) advertises standards for 73 countries. Its public CountryCode selector actually had 73 options: Common Sizes, European Union, and 71 named countries/regions. It offers country → photo type → print size, including 4×6 and single-photo output. We benchmark offerings only; its measurements are not evidence for official requirements. Our photo processing remains entirely local.

Coverage of named benchmark countries/regions: **54/71**. Additional named countries/regions: **41**.

Benchmark entries without a compatible verified printed route in this release: Albania, Belize, Bulgaria, Colombia, Iran, Jamaica, Kenya, Malaysia, Netherlands, Pakistan, Panama, Spain, Tanzania, Thailand, Trinidad & Tobago, Venezuela, Vietnam. See regional exclusion notes for reasons; omission does not imply no valid photo route exists.

Additional countries/regions: Algeria, Angola, Anguilla, Antigua & Barbuda, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bahrain, Bhutan, Bosnia & Herzegovina, British Virgin Islands, Cambodia, Dominica, Dominican Republic, Egypt, Ghana, Grenada, Haiti, Iceland, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Mauritius, Moldova, Montenegro, Mozambique, Myanmar (Burma), Namibia, Nepal, Oman, Papua New Guinea, Paraguay, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Sri Lanka, St. Kitts & Nevis, Tunisia, Turkmenistan, Zambia.

## Verification boundaries

- Included only numerical sizes supported by government, embassy/consulate or authorized application-operator publications. No generic Schengen size is assigned to every member.
- A printed-photo checklist remains useful when live biometrics are also required; the preset prepares the requested paper photograph and does not replace the appointment.
- The tool exports fixed 300-ppi 4×6 sheets. Dutch passport/visa sources require at least400dpi and Greek passport rules at least1200dpi; incompatible routes were excluded. No increase in export resolution was silently introduced.
- Photographer-only/approved-booth and exclusively digital/live-capture routes were excluded where no compatible paper route was verified. Australia and New Zealand can use an image prepared at home but demand specific printing processes/materials; those requirements are visible.
- Numeric measurements/background/recency absent from a source were not copied from another country. When an allowed dimension range exists, the chosen size is within it.
- Mandatory appearance restrictions default to neutral adjustments. The tool does not certify background, pose, identity, studio provenance, signatures on the back, or agency acceptance.
- Head guides are optional centred size references, not a full biometric template. China and Finland have asymmetric top/bottom spacing rules; measurements remain in text and centred guides are omitted for those routes.
- Some official sites block automated retrieval. Where direct opening failed but a complete official-domain extraction was available, that primary publication was used; sources with expired application windows or missing measurements were replaced or omitted. A check date records research, not a guarantee that a source will remain reachable.
